The Operation.

Everything the frame describes is a single move performed seven times: taking something that could not be substituted and re-engineering it into a unit that can. Fungible things can be priced, moved, and administered. Particular things resist.

The ledger below runs the operation on its own. Watch it, or take the control at the bottom of the screen and run it by hand — all seven domains convert at once.
